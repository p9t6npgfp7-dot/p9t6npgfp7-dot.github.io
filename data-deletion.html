Data Deletion

Jesse Liao Release Tool · Facebook and Instagram · Home · Privacy Policy

What the tool keeps

The tool is used only by its owner, Jesse Liao, and keeps data only on the owner's computer: the access tokens for the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account (encrypted with Windows DPAPI), the Page and Instagram account IDs and names, and the IDs, links, captions and status of Reels it published. It does not collect data about other Facebook or Instagram users.

How to delete it

  1. Remove the app's access in Facebook settings → Business integrations. The stored tokens stop working at once, and the tool stops publishing to that Page and Instagram account.
  2. Delete the tool's data folder on the owner's computer. This removes the stored tokens, IDs and release records.
  3. Reels already published stay on Facebook and Instagram until they are deleted there.
